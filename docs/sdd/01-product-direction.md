# Product Direction

## Vision
AgentHire is an evidence-driven AI job application copilot that maintains a structured knowledge base about the candidate and uses that information to analyze job opportunities and prepare personalized, human-reviewed applications.

## Core Principle
**EVIDENCE BEFORE CHANGE.** 
The candidate's profile is not just a CV, but a structured knowledge base where every skill and experience is backed by evidence. 
AgentHire must NOT invent or fabricate information (No Fabrication Principle).

## Target Flow
1. **Candidate Knowledge Base**: Structured, evidence-backed data.
2. **Job Source**: Scrape/import from LinkedIn, etc.
3. **Job Analysis**: Extract requirements and keywords.
4. **Candidate Match**: Compare Job Requirements against Candidate Knowledge Base based on Evidence.
5. **Personalization**: Generate tailored CVs and cover letters from the exact evidence match.
6. **Human Review**: Candidate reviews and approves.
7. **Application**: Assisted or automated submission.
